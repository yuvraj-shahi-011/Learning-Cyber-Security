🛡️ Risk Treatment

Once a risk is identified, an organization generally has several options.

1. Risk Avoidance

Stop the activity that creates the risk.

Example: Stop using an insecure application.

2. Risk Mitigation

Take security measures to reduce the likelihood or impact.

Example: Add MFA to protect accounts.

3. Risk Transfer

Transfer some of the financial consequences or responsibility to another party.

Example: Cyber insurance.

4. Risk Acceptance

Understand the risk and consciously accept it within the organization's risk tolerance.

Example: A low-impact risk may not justify the cost of additional controls.
